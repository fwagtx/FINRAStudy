# SIE Cheat Sheet — Numbers, Formulas & Dates

## Formulas

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| Outstanding shares | Issued − Treasury |
| Current yield (bond or stock) | Annual income ÷ Market price |
| Tax-equivalent yield (muni) | Muni yield ÷ (1 − tax bracket) |
| Conversion ratio | Par ÷ Conversion price |
| Parity price of bond/preferred | Stock price × Conversion ratio |
| Parity price of stock | Bond price ÷ Conversion ratio |
| NAV per share | (Assets − Liabilities) ÷ Shares outstanding |
| POP (front-end load fund) | NAV ÷ (1 − Sales charge %) |
| Sales charge % | (POP − NAV) ÷ POP |
| Expense ratio | Annual expenses ÷ Average net assets |
| EPS | (Net income − Preferred dividends) ÷ Common shares |
| P/E | Price ÷ EPS |
| Dividend payout ratio | Dividends per share ÷ EPS |
| Current ratio | Current assets ÷ Current liabilities |
| Working capital | Current assets − Current liabilities |
| Long margin equity | LMV − Debit balance |
| Short margin equity | Credit balance − SMV |
| Long maintenance call point | Debit ÷ 0.75 |
| Short maintenance call point | Credit ÷ 1.30 |
| Buying power | SMA × 2 |
| Rule 144 volume limit | Greater of 1% outstanding or 4-week avg weekly volume (per 90 days) |
| CAPM expected return | Rf + β × (Rm − Rf) |
| Alpha | Actual return − CAPM expected return |
| Split (A-for-B) | Shares × A/B; price × B/A |
| Accrued interest (corp/muni) | Coupon × days/360 (30-day months) |

## Options (per share; ×100)

| Position | Max gain | Max loss | Breakeven |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long call | Unlimited | Premium | Strike + premium |
| Short call | Premium | Unlimited (naked) | Strike + premium |
| Long put | Strike − premium | Premium | Strike − premium |
| Short put | Premium | Strike − premium | Strike − premium |
| Covered call | (Strike − cost) + premium | Cost − premium | Cost − premium |
| Protective put | Unlimited | (Cost − strike) + premium | Cost + premium |

Call Up / Put Down. Even split → more contracts, lower strike. Uneven split → more shares per contract. No adjustment for cash dividends. Auto-exercise at $0.01 ITM. Index options: cash settled, $100 multiplier, usually European.

## Yield relationships

- Discount bond: Nominal < CY < YTM < YTC
- Premium bond: Nominal > CY > YTM > YTC
- Rates ↑ → prices ↓. Long maturity & low coupon = most volatile. Zeros: most volatile, no reinvestment risk, phantom income.
- Lowest investment grade: **BBB / Baa**.

## Settlement & payment

| Item | Timing |
|---|---|
| Stocks, corporate & muni bonds, Treasuries, options, mutual funds | **T+1** |
| Reg T payment | **S+2** (T+3); else sell-out + 90-day freeze |
| Ex-dividend date | Same day as record date (T+1 world) |
| Mutual fund redemption paid | Within **7 days** |
| ACATS | Validate 1 business day; complete 3 business days after |
| Tender offer open | ≥ 20 business days |
| Options agreement returned | Within 15 days of approval |

## Margin

Reg T **50%** (Fed). FINRA: min deposit **$2,000**; maintenance **25% long / 30% short**. Rehypothecation **140%** of debit. Pattern day trader **$25,000**. Mutual funds/IPOs not marginable for **30 days**. No margin: custodial, IRA, most trusts.

## Mutual funds

Max sales charge **8.5%** (needs breakpoints + ROA + reinvestment at NAV). No-load → 12b-1 ≤ **0.25%**; 12b-1 max **1.00%**. LOI **13 months**, backdate **90 days**. Diversified = **75-5-10**. Subchapter M: distribute ≥ **90%**. Board ≥ **40%** independent. $100,000 net worth to go public. Class A = front load (big/long); B = CDSC → converts to A; C = level (short-term). REIT: 90% distribution, 75% real estate, no loss pass-through.

## Primary market

Cooling-off ≥ **20 days**. Prospectus delivery after IPO: **90 days** non-listed, **25 days** listed; follow-on non-listed **40 days**. Green shoe **15%**. Reg D 506(b): unlimited accredited + **35** non-accredited. Accredited: **$1M net worth** (ex-home) or **$200k/$300k** income. Reg A: **$20M / $75M**. Crowdfunding **$5M**. Rule 147: 6-month resale lock. 144A: QIBs ≥ **$100M**. Rule 144: **6-month** hold; Form 144 valid **90 days**; de minimis ≤ **5,000 shares & ≤ $50,000**. Commercial paper ≤ **270 days** (exempt). Penny stock < **$5** unlisted. Shelf: **3 years**. Rule 5130: no IPOs to restricted persons.

## Municipal

TEY = yield ÷ (1 − bracket). G-37: **$250**/election to candidates you can vote for, else **2-year** ban. G-20 gifts **$300**. G.O. = taxes, voter approval, debt limits; revenue = project, feasibility study, coverage ratio. Notes: TAN, RAN, TRAN, BAN, GAN. Territory bonds triple tax-free. Private activity → AMT. Not for IRAs.

## Government

Treasuries: fed taxable, state exempt; quoted 1/32nds; $100 min; actual/365. Bills ≤ 52 wks (discount); notes 2–10 yrs; bonds 20–30 yrs. Ginnie Mae = only full-faith agency; MBS taxed at all levels; prepayment risk when rates fall. Fed tools: open-market ops (most used; buy = ease), discount rate, reserve requirements (least used). Fed funds = bank-to-bank overnight; discount = Fed to banks; prime = banks to best customers; broker call = banks to BDs. Recession = 2 quarters negative GDP. Leading: stock market, housing starts, M2, claims. Lagging: unemployment, CPI, prime rate. Fiscal = Congress/President (taxes, spending).

## Taxes

LT > 1 year: 0/15/20%. Net loss deduction **$3,000**/yr, carry forward. Wash sale **30 days** before/after. Gift: donor's basis carries. Inheritance: **stepped-up**, auto long-term. Gift exclusion **$19,000** (2026). Backup withholding **24%**. Qualified dividends = LT rates. Passive losses only vs. passive income. Earned income needed for IRA.

## Retirement (2026)

IRA **$7,500** + **$1,100** catch-up (50+); deadline April 15; excess 6%. 401(k) **$24,500** + **$8,000** (50+) / **$11,250** (60–63); total **$72,000**. SIMPLE **$17,000** (≤ 100 employees; 3% match or 2%; 25% penalty first 2 yrs). SEP employer-only 25%/$72,000. Early penalty **10%** before **59½**. RMD at **73** (75 if born 1960+); 25% penalty. Roth: 5 years + 59½, no RMD, income limits. Rollover **60 days**, once/12 months, **20%** withholding on plan checks. ERISA: age 21 + 1 year; employee money always vested. 457 = no 10% penalty. 403(b) = schools/non-profits. DB favors older; DC favors younger. 529: $19k/yr or $95k 5-yr election, owner controls, 10% on non-qualified earnings. Coverdell **$2,000**/yr, income limits, until 18, use by 30. VA: AIR drives payment changes; straight life = highest; LIFO; M&E charge; 1035 exchange tax-free. VL: 24-month exchange right, 9% max sales charge.

## Accounts

Required: name, address, DOB, SSN, rep, principal approval, other-BD employment, insider status. Trusted contact (attempt). Account record copy every **36 months**. JTWROS → survivor; TIC → estate. UGMA/UTMA: one custodian/one minor, irrevocable, minor's SSN, no margin. Discretionary: written authority + principal approval; time/price only ≠ discretion. Statements quarterly; confirms by settlement. Complaints kept **4 years**; arbitration eligibility **6 years**. SAR **$5,000**/30 days; CTR **> $10,000** cash. **SIPC $500,000 / $250,000 cash**, per separate customer, not market losses. FDIC $250,000. BCP: annual review, 2 emergency contacts.

## Orders

Buy limit & sell stop **below** market; sell limit & buy stop **above** (BLiSS/SLoBS). Stop → market order when triggered; stop-limit → limit order (may not fill). Orders below market reduced on ex-date unless DNR. Order tickets kept 3 years. Circuit breakers: 7%/13% → 15 min (not after 3:25); 20% → day. Reg SHO short-sale restriction at −10%. Best execution regardless of payment for order flow.

## Rules & ethics

Insider trading: treble damages; **$5M / 20 years** (firms $25M). Senior hold: **15 + 10** business days; specified adult 65+ or impaired 18+. TCPA: **8 am–9 pm** called party's time; national DNC check every 31 days; 18-month relationship exemption. Gifts **$300**/person/year (was $100 before March 2026). OBA: prior written notice. Private securities transactions: notice; approval if paid. Outside accounts: employer consent. Borrowing/lending: family, institution, same-firm rep, personal/business relationship (with procedures). Sharing accounts: written approval + proportional (except family). Never guarantee against loss.
Communications: correspondence ≤ 25 retail/30 days; retail > 25 → principal pre-approval; new firm files 10 business days **before** use; fund/VA/DPP/CMO filed within 10 business days **after** first use; institutional → procedures. Performance 1/5/10 yr at max load. Records 3 years.
Licensing: SIE 18+, no sponsor, 75 Q / 105 min / 70 pass / 4 yrs valid; fails wait 30/30/180. U4 changes 30 days; U5 30 days; lapse after 2 yrs (MQP 5). CE: Regulatory Element annually (inactive if missed); Firm Element annual needs analysis. Statutory disqualification: felony/securities misdemeanor within 10 years.
Records: permanent (org docs, minutes); **6 yrs** (blotters, ledgers, stock record, account records); **4 yrs** (complaints); **3 yrs** (tickets, confirms, communications, U4/U5 post-termination, gifts, net capital); first 2 years easily accessible.
Regulators: SEC (government, 1934 Act); FINRA (SRO, BDs/reps); MSRB (rules only, no enforcement, no issuers); states/NASAA (blue-sky, Series 63/65/66); CFTC/NFA (futures); FinCEN (AML); SIPC; FDIC. Reg BI: disclosure, care, conflicts, compliance; Form CRS. IAs = fiduciary.

# Chapter 14: Retirement & Education Plans

> **In one line:** Every plan is either "deduct now, tax later" or "tax now, tax-free later" — know who qualifies, the limits, the penalties, and how variable products pay.

## The big ideas

1. **Pre-tax in → ordinary income out; after-tax in → tax-free out** — this one split answers most tax questions.
2. **59½ and 73 bracket the withdrawal window** — before 59½ costs a 10% penalty (with exceptions); RMDs start at 73, except for Roth IRA owners.
3. **Who bears the investment risk decides the product** — employer (DB pension), insurer (fixed annuity, whole life) or customer (DC plan, variable annuity, variable life).
4. **Variable products are securities and insurance** — prospectus, separate account, and both licenses.
5. **Don't double up tax shelters** — munis or annuities inside an IRA waste the tax break.

## Must-know facts

### Plan types
- **Qualified** — non-discriminatory, pre-tax, all taxable out. **Non-qualified** — can favor executives; only earnings taxed.
- **ERISA** — private-sector plans only (not government or IRAs); age 21 + 1 year; employee money always vested.

| | **Defined benefit** | **Defined contribution** |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed | Benefit at retirement | Money going in |
| Risk | Employer | Employee |
| Favors | Older, highly paid | Younger |

- **403(b)** — public schools, hospitals, 501(c)(3)s; annuities and mutual funds only.
- **457** — state/local government; no 10% penalty after separation.
- **Profit-sharing** — contributions discretionary. **Money purchase** — fixed % required yearly.
- **SEP** — employer-only contributions. **SIMPLE** — ≤ 100 employees, no other plan; 25% penalty in first 2 years.

### IRAs
- **Earned income only** — wages, self-employment; not dividends, interest, rent, pensions or Social Security.
- **Traditional** — deductible unless covered by an employer plan **and** income too high (then non-deductible).
- **Roth** — never deductible; income limits; contributions out anytime tax-free; earnings tax-free if 5 years **and** 59½; no owner RMDs.
- **Conversion** — taxed as ordinary income, no 10% penalty.
- **Not allowed** — collectibles, life insurance, margin. **Unsuitable** — munis.
- **Rollover** — 60 days, once per 12 months; plan checks carry 20% withholding. **Direct transfer** — unlimited, no withholding.

### Variable life
- **Guaranteed** — minimum death benefit (if premiums paid). **Not guaranteed** — cash value.
- **Exchange right** — swap to fixed whole life within 24 months, no insurability evidence. **Sales charge cap** — 9%.
- **1035 exchanges** — life→life, life→annuity, annuity→annuity; **not annuity→life**.

### Annuities
| | **Fixed** | **Variable** |
|---|---|---|
| Risk borne by | Insurer (owner keeps inflation risk) | Owner |
| Security? | No | Yes |

- **Accumulation units** → **annuity units** at annuitization (irrevocable).
- **AIR** — return above AIR → next payment rises; below → falls; equal → unchanged.
- **Payouts** — **life only** = highest payment; **joint and last survivor** = lowest.
- **Taxes** — withdrawals LIFO (earnings first, ordinary income); 10% penalty on earnings before 59½.
- **Death benefit** — at least premiums paid (less withdrawals).

### Education and other
| | **529** | **Coverdell ESA** |
|---|---|---|
| Contribution | Gift-tax limit; 5-year front-load | $2,000/beneficiary/yr |
| Income limits | None | Yes |
| Age limits | None | Contribute to 18, use by 30 |
| Control | Account owner | Custodian |

- **529** — tax-free for qualified education; owner may change beneficiary within the family; MSRB rules apply.

## Formulas

```
Next variable annuity payment: return > AIR → up; < AIR → down; = AIR → same
```
AIR 4%: earns 6% → payment rises; earns 4% → unchanged; earns 3% → payment falls.

```
Full rollover = gross distribution (check + 20% withheld)
```
$100,000 distribution → $80,000 check; redeposit $100,000 within 60 days.

## Exam traps

- **Trap:** Annuity payments fall when returns drop → **Truth:** they fall only when returns are below the AIR.
- **Trap:** Pre-tax plan withdrawals of stock gains are capital gains → **Truth:** always ordinary income.
- **Trap:** Roth IRAs require RMDs at 73 → **Truth:** no lifetime RMDs for Roth owners.
- **Trap:** Covered by a 401(k) means no traditional IRA → **Truth:** you can contribute; it may just be non-deductible.
- **Trap:** Variable life guarantees cash value → **Truth:** only the minimum death benefit is guaranteed.
- **Trap:** Annuity → life insurance is a 1035 exchange → **Truth:** it is not allowed.
- **Trap:** The 529 beneficiary controls the account → **Truth:** the owner (donor) keeps control.

## Memory hooks

- **"Life alone pays most; two lives pay least."**
- **"AIR is the bar"** — beat it, payment up; miss it, payment down.
- **Roth "5 and 59½"** — both must be met for tax-free earnings.
- **"Annuity can't go back to life"** — 1035 is one-way from annuities.

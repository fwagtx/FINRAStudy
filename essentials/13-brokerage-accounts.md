# Chapter 13: Brokerage Accounts

> **In one line:** Know who owns and who controls each account, what paperwork unlocks margin, options and discretion, how margin equity moves, and what SIPC does and doesn't protect.

## The big ideas

1. **Ownership and authority are separate** — a minor, estate or trust may own the account while a custodian, executor or trustee gives orders, each with a specific document.
2. **More risk, more paperwork** — cash accounts need no customer signature; margin, options and discretion each need signed agreements plus principal approval.
3. **Margin is a fixed loan** — the debit (or credit) balance stays put, so every price move hits equity dollar for dollar.
4. **Fiduciary accounts can't speculate** — custodial, IRA and most trust accounts bar margin.
5. **SIPC replaces missing assets, never lost value** — it covers a failed broker-dealer, not a falling market.

## Must-know facts

### Records and opening
- **Statements** — at least quarterly. **Confirms** — at or before settlement.
- **Complaint** — must be **written** (email counts); to a principal; kept 4 years; rep never settles privately.
- **Required info** — name, residential address, DOB/legal age, SSN/TIN, rep's name, **principal's signature**. Customer signature **not** required for cash.
- **Investment profile** — sought, but customer may refuse; account can still open.
- **Trusted contact** — firm must *attempt* to get one; no account authority.
- **Employee of another member** — prior written notice to the employer.
- **AML** — SAR for suspicious $5,000+ within 30 days (don't tip off); CTR for cash over $10,000; placement → layering → integration.

### Registrations
| | **JTWROS** | **Tenants in common** |
|---|---|---|
| Shares | Equal | Can be unequal |
| At a death | To survivor(s) | To deceased's **estate** |

- **Joint checks** — payable to all owners.
- **Limited POA** — trade only. **Full POA** — trade + withdraw. **Durable** survives incapacity; **all end at death**.
- **Discretion** — rep picks security, action or amount → written authorization first, principal approval, prompt review. Time/price alone is **not** discretion.
- **UGMA/UTMA** — one custodian, one minor, minor's SSN, **irrevocable** gift, no margin.
- **Documents** — guardian: court papers; trust: trust document (margin only if allowed); corporation: resolution, plus charter for margin.

### Margin
- **Agreements** — credit (interest), **hypothecation** (pledge; rehypothecate ≤ 140% of debit), **loan consent** (optional).
- **Not marginable** — new issues/mutual funds for 30 days, most options, penny stocks.
- **Reg T call** — cash, or fully paid securities worth **2×** the call; unmet → sell-out and 90-day freeze.
- **SMA** — excess equity; buying power = 2 × SMA.

### Options and SIPC
- **Options account** — ODD at or before approval → ROP approves → agreement signed within 15 days, else closing trades only.
- **SIPC** — non-government; per **separate customer** (individual, joint, IRA, custodial each separate; two individual accounts in one name combine).
- **Not covered** — market losses, commodities/futures, fixed annuities. **FDIC** covers bank deposits.
- **BCP** — annual review, summary disclosed at opening.

## Formulas

```
Long:  Equity = LMV − Debit      Short: Equity = Credit − SMV
```
Short $10,000 + $5,000 deposit → CR $15,000; stock falls to $8,000 → EQ $7,000.

```
Deposit = greater of 50% or $2,000, never more than the price
```
Buy $3,000 → $2,000; buy $1,500 → $1,500.

```
SMA = Equity − 50% × LMV;  Buying power = 2 × SMA
```
LMV $24,000, DR $10,000 → $14,000 − $12,000 = $2,000 → $4,000.

```
Long call trigger = Debit ÷ 0.75     Short call trigger = Credit ÷ 1.30
```
DR $15,000 ÷ 0.75 → call below $20,000 LMV.

```
Maintenance call = 25% × LMV − Equity
```
LMV $12,000, DR $10,000 → $3,000 − $2,000 = $1,000.

## Exam traps

- **Trap:** The customer must sign a cash account form → **Truth:** only the principal's approval signature is required.
- **Trap:** TIC passes to the co-owner → **Truth:** TIC goes to the estate; JTWROS goes to the survivor.
- **Trap:** A durable POA survives death → **Truth:** durable survives incapacity; every POA ends at death.
- **Trap:** "You pick the price" needs written discretion → **Truth:** time/price alone is not discretion.
- **Trap:** A $3,000 Reg T call is met with $3,000 of stock → **Truth:** securities must be worth twice the call.
- **Trap:** SIPC covers market losses → **Truth:** never; only missing assets of a failed firm.
- **Trap:** SIPC covers $500,000 cash → **Truth:** $500,000 total, only $250,000 cash.

## Memory hooks

- **JTWROS = "With Rights Of Survivor"** — survivor wins; TIC → estate.
- **"Limited trades, full fetches funds."**
- **Margin papers "Credit, Hock, Lend"** — only Lend is optional.
- **Options "D-A-S":** Disclose (ODD) → Approve (ROP) → Sign (15 days).
- **"0.75 below, call below"** — long call when LMV < Debit ÷ 0.75.

# Chapter 15: Rules & Ethics

> **In one line:** Know who regulates what, the bright-line prohibitions, the "notice vs. approval" rep rules, and the numbers behind communications, licensing and records.

## The big ideas

1. **SEC is government; FINRA and MSRB are SROs** — the MSRB writes muni rules but can't enforce them or touch issuers.
2. **Anything that fakes price or volume is manipulation** — the lone legal exception is a disclosed stabilizing bid at or below the offering price.
3. **Notice vs. approval** — outside jobs need written notice; paid private securities deals and outside brokerage accounts need the firm's written approval/consent.
4. **Audience size decides communication rules** — 25 or fewer retail in 30 days is correspondence; more is retail communication needing principal pre-approval.
5. **Retail recommendations = Reg BI** — best interest, Form CRS, and conflicts disclosed or mitigated; advisers owe a full fiduciary duty.

## Must-know facts

### Regulators
- **SEC** — federal agency (1934 Act); civil actions; criminal cases go to DOJ.
- **FINRA** — non-government SRO; exams, CRD, BrokerCheck, arbitration. Appeals: hearing panel → NAC → SEC → federal court.
- **MSRB** — rules enforced by FINRA, SEC and bank regulators.
- **State Administrator** — blue-sky laws (Uniform Securities Act); NASAA writes Series 63/65/66.

### Prohibited activities
| Practice | Key feature |
|---|---|
| **Wash trade** | No change in beneficial ownership |
| **Matched orders** | Two parties coordinate trades |
| **Front-running** | Trading ahead of a customer block or research report |
| **Spoofing** | Orders never meant to fill |
| **Marking the close** | Trading near the close to set the price |
| **Freeriding** | Selling before paying in a cash account → 90-day freeze |

- **Churning** — excessive trading measured by the rep's commissions vs. the customer's profile.
- **Guarantee against loss** — always prohibited.
- **Sharing in an account** — firm + customer written approval; proportional to the rep's investment (family exempt from proportionality).
- **Borrowing/lending** — only if firm procedures allow and the customer is immediate family, a lending institution, a same-firm rep, or an outside personal/business relationship.

### Insider trading
- **MNPI** — material + non-public; tipper liable even without trading; tippees down the chain liable.
- **Penalties** — civil up to 3× profit gained/loss avoided; criminal up to 20 years and $5 million ($25 million firms).
- **Not insider trading** — public info, rumors, mosaic analysis, 10b5-1 plans.
- **Firm duties** — information barriers, watch/restricted lists.

### Seniors and privacy
- **Specified adult** — 65+, or 18+ with an impairment.
- **Temporary hold** — 15 business days + 10 (+30 if a regulator asks); notify trusted contact within 2 business days.

### Rep rules
| Situation | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Outside business activity** (3270) | Prior written **notice** |
| **Private securities transaction, paid** (3280) | Notice + written **approval**; firm supervises |
| **Private securities transaction, unpaid** | Notice; firm acknowledges |
| **Account at another BD** (3210) | Employer's prior written **consent**; duplicates on request |

- **Gifts (3220)** — $300 per person per year; entertainment excluded if the giver attends.
- **Reg BI** — Disclosure, Care, Conflict of interest, Compliance; Form CRS by the earliest of recommendation, order or account opening; eliminate sales contests on specific securities.

### Communications (Rule 2210)
| Type | Audience | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| **Correspondence** | ≤ 25 retail in 30 days | Review (not pre-approval) |
| **Retail** | > 25 retail in 30 days | Principal **before** use |
| **Institutional** | Institutions only | Written procedures |

- **Filing** — new firm: 10 business days **before** first use (first year); funds/VAs/options/DPPs: within 10 business days **after**.
- **Content** — fair and balanced; no predictions or guarantees; fund returns for 1, 5, 10 years.
- **TCPA** — 8 a.m.–9 p.m. **called party's** time; firm do-not-call list kept 5 years.
- **Proxies** — firm forwards (issuer pays); uninstructed shares voted only on routine matters.

### Licensing and CE
- **SIE** — 18+, no sponsor; does not register anyone; valid 4 years.
- **Series 6** — funds/VAs; **7** — general; **24** — principal; **63/65/66** — state.
- **U4/U5** — amend within 30 days; U5 within 30 days of leaving.
- **Regulatory Element** — annual; miss it → registration inactive.
- **Statutory disqualification** — any felony, or securities/money misdemeanor, within 10 years.

## Exam traps

- **Trap:** The MSRB fines dealers that break its rules → **Truth:** FINRA, the SEC or bank regulators enforce MSRB rules.
- **Trap:** A tipper who didn't trade is off the hook → **Truth:** passing MNPI is itself a violation.
- **Trap:** A paid weekend job needs firm approval → **Truth:** OBAs need prior written notice; the firm may then restrict them.
- **Trap:** An email to 20 retail customers needs principal pre-approval → **Truth:** that's correspondence — review only.
- **Trap:** Cold calls are timed by the caller's clock → **Truth:** use the called party's local time.
- **Trap:** Passing the SIE lets you sell securities → **Truth:** you must also pass a rep exam and be registered.
- **Trap:** A $300 dinner the rep attends counts toward the gift limit → **Truth:** attended business entertainment is excluded.

## Memory hooks

- **"Notice the job, approve the pay"** — OBA = notice; paid private deal = approval.
- **"25 or less, just review the rest"** — correspondence vs. retail.
- **"Eight to nine, their time"** — TCPA.
- **Records "Forever-6-4-3":** organization forever, books 6, complaints 4, everything else 3.
- **Reg BI "D-C-C-C":** Disclosure, Care, Conflicts, Compliance.
